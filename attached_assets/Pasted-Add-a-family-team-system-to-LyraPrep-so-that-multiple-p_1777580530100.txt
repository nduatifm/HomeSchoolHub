Add a family team system to LyraPrep so that multiple parents or guardians can share access to a child's account. This replaces the current single-parent foreign key (Student.parentId) with a many-to-many join table. Do not add new features beyond what is specified below. Work through the implementation in the order listed.
1. Database migration

Create a new child_team_members join table with the following columns: id, child_id (foreign key to Student), parent_id (foreign key to Parent/User), role (enum: owner or member), status (enum: active or pending), invited_by (foreign key to the Parent who sent the invite), invited_at (timestamp), accepted_at (timestamp, nullable), created_at
Migrate all existing Student.parentId values into the new child_team_members table as owner / active records before dropping the Student.parentId column. No existing parent should lose access during or after migration
Verify all existing parent-facing queries that previously joined on Student.parentId are updated to query through the new join table

2. Roles and permissions

Two roles exist on a team: Owner and Member. Multiple team members can hold the Owner role simultaneously
Owners can: invite new members, change any member's role, remove members, send messages to the tutor, and perform all management actions (inviting students, managing the child's team)
Members can: view classrooms, grades, assignments, submissions, classroom notifications, and read message threads. Members cannot send messages, invite new members, or manage the team
All parent-facing read routes (classrooms, grades, submissions, notifications, messaging) must accept any active team member — Owner or Member
Management routes (invite, role change, remove member) must enforce the Owner role and reject Member sessions with a clear error notification
A team must always have at least one Owner. If only one Owner remains, the UI must block demoting or removing them until another Member is promoted to Owner first. Show a clear inline error explaining why the action is blocked

3. Invite flow

On the "My Children" page, each child card has a "Team" section showing avatars and names of all active team members with their role badge (Owner / Member)
An Owner can click "Invite to team" and enter an email address. On submit, the system sends a magic link invite email to that address. The magic link expires after 24 hours
Pending invites appear in the Team panel immediately with a "Pending" badge. The Owner can cancel or resend a pending invite at any time
If the invitee clicks the link before it expires and already has a LyraPrep parent account, they are prompted to log in and access is granted on login
If the invitee does not have an account, they land on a streamlined signup flow and access is granted on completion
If the invitee clicks an expired link, show a clear message explaining the link has expired with an option to contact the Owner to request a new invite — not a generic 404
On acceptance, the invite status updates from pending to active and accepted_at is recorded. The inviting Owner receives an in-app notification that the invite was accepted

4. Team management

An Owner can change any Member's role to Owner. Since multiple Owners are allowed, this does not affect any existing Owner's role — it is additive only
An Owner can change any other Owner's role to Member, provided at least one other Owner remains on the team. If they are the last Owner, the action is blocked with a clear inline message: "At least one Owner is required. Promote another member to Owner before making this change"
An Owner can remove any Member from the team
An Owner can remove another Owner from the team, subject to the same last-Owner guard above
All role changes and removals take effect immediately and the affected member's session is updated on their next page load

5. Member experience

Shared children appear on the Member's "My Children" page alongside their own children
Each shared child card shows a "Member" badge and a "Shared by [Owner name]" sub-label so the Member knows who manages the team
Members see the full read view for classrooms, grades, assignments, submissions, and the message thread — identical to what an Owner sees, minus the action controls (no send message button, no team management panel)

6. Notifications

All active team members — Owner and Member — receive both in-app and email notifications for classroom activity, new assignments, grade updates, and tutor messages
Notification delivery preferences (in-app only, email only, both) can be managed per team member from the Team panel by any Owner
The invite accepted notification is delivered in-app to the Owner who sent the invite

7. Messaging permissions

Only Owners can send messages in the three-way thread (Teacher · Student · Parent)
Members can read the full thread history but the composer is hidden from their view
If a child has multiple Owners, all Owners can send messages into the same shared thread. Messages from different Owners are attributed by name so the teacher can see who sent each message

8. Notifications and error handling

All success, error, and warning states use the existing unified in-app notification system (top center, auto-dismiss for success, persist for errors)
No browser alerts, confirms, or raw error strings should be exposed to the user at any point in this flow
Empty states: if a child has no team members other than the Owner, the Team panel shows a friendly prompt to invite a co-parent or guardian rather than an empty list

9. End-to-end smoke test after implementation

Log in as an existing parent and confirm their child access is intact after the parentId migration
As an Owner, invite a new email, accept the invite from the invitee side, and confirm the Member appears in the Team panel with the correct badge
Confirm the Member can view classrooms, grades, and the message thread but cannot send messages or manage the team
Promote the Member to Owner and confirm both users now have Owner-level access
Attempt to remove the last remaining Owner and confirm the action is blocked with the correct inline message
Confirm all in-app and email notifications fire correctly for both Owner and Member accounts
Confirm no existing parent-facing functionality is broken by the parentId migration
