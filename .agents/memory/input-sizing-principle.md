---
name: Input sizing principle
description: User-approved usability direction for editing, choosing, and reviewing content across roles
---

Use purpose-based sizing: keep dates, scores, codes, filters, and short choices compact but comfortably tappable; let sustained writing expand within a viewport-safe limit; make read-only answers easy to scan without pretending they are editable.

**Why:** The user explicitly questioned whether simply enlarging all boxes was best and accepted a purpose-based approach. Blanket enlargement wastes space and makes dense grading and scheduling forms harder to use.

**How to apply:** When changing student, teacher, or parent forms, choose field dimensions based on expected content and available width. Preserve validation, autosave, and submitted values; check keyboard labels, focus, and small-screen layouts alongside visual spacing.
